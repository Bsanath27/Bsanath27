# ⚖️ Regulatory Compliance: Safety Standards

Operating a fruit business in India requires strict adherence to FSSAI (Food Safety and Standards Authority of India) guidelines, especially regarding ripening agents.

## 🚫 Banned Substances
- **Calcium Carbide (Masala):** Strictly prohibited under FSSAI Regulations, 2011. 
- **Hazards:** Contains traces of arsenic and phosphorus. Can cause dizziness, nausea, and skin ulcers.

## ✅ Approved Ripening (Ethylene)
- **Ethylene Gas:** Permitted at concentrations up to **100 ppm**.
- **Enripe Status:** Fully compliant with FSSAI (F.No.Stds/F&VP/Artificial Ripening/01/FSSAI-2017).
- **Labeling:** Fruits ripened with ethylene must be declared as such by reputable stores/dealers.

## 🏛️ Export Quality Standards (Benchmarks)
To compete with premium exporters, your store should follow these standards:
- **Traceability:** Farm-to-fork tracking.
- **Residue Compliance:** Testing for pesticide levels (European market benchmark).
- **Cold Chain Integrity:** Maintaining temperatures between **20-22°C** during ripening.

## 📦 Operational Compliance
- **Packhouse Standards:** Use HACCP (Hazard Analysis Critical Control Point) certified packing environments.
- **Phytosanitary Clearance:** Essential if you expand beyond local e-commerce to inter-state or export.

> [!important] Mandatory Labeling
> Declare clearly on your Shopify store and packaging: "Naturally Ripened using FSSAI-Approved Ethylene Technology. Zero Calcium Carbide."
