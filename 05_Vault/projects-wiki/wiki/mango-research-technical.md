# 🛠️ Technical Feasibility: Enripe Solution

The business model relies on **Enripe (en-ripe)** ethylene-based sachets to trigger natural ripening in a controlled environment.

## 🧬 The Technology
- **Ripening Agent:** Ethylene gas (naturally produced by fruits).
- **Safety:** 100% chemical-free and FSSAI approved.
- **Form Factor:** Small sachets placed inside crates (10kg capacity).
- **Ripening Success Rate:** 99.9%.

## 🥭 Mango Ripening Specifics
- **Ripening Time:** 60 - 84 hours (approx. 3.5 days).
- **Procurement State:** "Matured Raw" (harvested at full maturity but still green/hard).
- **Yield:** Consistent uniform color and taste compared to traditional methods.

## 📋 Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)
1. **Procurement:** Buy matured-raw mangoes directly from farms.
2. **Setup:** Use ventilated plastic crates (10kg capacity).
3. **Activation:** Punch the red circle on the Enripe sachet to release ethylene.
4. **Packing:** Lay paper ➔ Place sachet ➔ Fill with mangoes ➔ Seal crate.
5. **Incubation:** Store in a well-ventilated warehouse for 60-84 hours.
6. **Quality Check:** Sort for peak ripeness and "aroma" signature.
7. **Delivery:** Last-mile delivery to customer.

## 🏗️ Requirements
- **Warehouse:** Well-ventilated space in Chennai.
- **Temperature Control:** Ideal mango ripening temperature is **20-22°C**.
- **Logistics:** Crates, newspaper/padding, and Enripe sachets.
