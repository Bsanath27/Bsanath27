**Enripe (en-ripe)** is India's leading natural fruit ripening solution company based in Hyderabad, Telangana. They use ethylene-based sachets for controlled fruit ripening, certified by FSSAI (Food Safety and Standards Authority of India).

- **Website**: https://www.enripe.com/#/

1. **All Fruits Sachet** - Universal solution (10kg capacity, 48-84 hour ripening)
2. **Mango Sachet** - Optimized for mangoes (10kg capacity, 84 hour ripening)
3. **Banana Sachet** - Specialized for bananas (10kg capacity, 48-72 hour ripening)
